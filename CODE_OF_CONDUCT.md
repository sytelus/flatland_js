# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Flatland should be a welcoming place to learn, experiment, and contribute. Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and other demeaning or unsafe conduct are not acceptable in project spaces or in private communication connected to the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repetition, and the safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns through a private contact method listed on the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/sytelus). If no private contact is available, open a minimal public issue asking a maintainer to contact you; do not include sensitive details. Maintainers should protect the reporter's privacy as far as reasonably possible and should recuse themselves when they have a conflict of interest.

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone is officially representing the project.
